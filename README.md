# Who I Am, What I've Been Through, and What I Want for My Future

My name is Kathryn Gividen, and I am someone who wants more out of life than simply getting by. ## My Goals and Ambitions

One of my biggest goals in life is to achieve financial freedom. I want to be able to support myself, provide stability for my family, enjoy the things I work hard for, and have the freedom to make decisions without constantly worrying about money. I don't necessarily know exactly what career path will get me there yet, but I know I want to make good money and build something meaningful for myself.

I am currently attending Southern New Hampshire University because I believe earning my degree will open doors and create opportunities that I might not otherwise have. I want my education to be an investment in my future. I have explored different career interests, including criminal justice and human services, but I am also open to discovering other opportunities that offer financial growth, independence, and long-term success.

I don't want to spend my life struggling just to make ends meet. I want to build wealth, become financially secure, and eventually have the ability to live life on my own terms.

## My Personality and Strengths

I consider myself a determined, compassionate, independent, and persistent person. I have experienced situations that have forced me to become stronger and learn how to keep going, even when things aren't going the way I planned.

I care about people and try to understand situations from different perspectives. I believe that everyone has a story, and I don't think someone should automatically be judged without understanding what they may have experienced or what circumstances they are facing.

I am also someone who thinks deeply about things. I reflect on my decisions, my relationships, my experiences, and the direction my life is heading. Sometimes I overthink, but I also believe that self-reflection is important because I want to continue growing and learning from my experiences.

I am independent and want to be able to depend on myself. I don't want to constantly have to ask someone else for help or feel like my future depends on another person's decisions.

## My Family and What Matters to Me

My family is important to me, especially my daughter. Being a mother has shaped how I look at life, responsibility, and the future. I want to create stability and opportunities that allow my family to have a better life.

I have experienced loss, disappointment, and difficult family circumstances. Losing my grandmother was especially difficult for me, and I have also experienced situations where I have felt like I needed to be strong for everyone else while trying to manage my own struggles.

I want to reach a point where I can enjoy life instead of constantly feeling like I am fighting to get through it. I want to have more positive experiences, build healthy relationships, and create memories with the people who matter to me.

## My Challenges and What I've Learned

I haven't had the easiest journey. There have been times when financial pressure, personal relationships, family responsibilities, and school have all felt overwhelming at once.

I have sometimes found myself putting my own needs and goals aside while trying to help other people. I have also experienced situations where I felt unappreciated, misunderstood, or taken for granted.

Through these experiences, I am learning that I cannot keep sacrificing my own well-being and future trying to make everything work for everyone else. I need to put more energy into myself, my education, my financial goals, and the future I want to create.

I am still learning how to establish boundaries, recognize my worth, and stop allowing circumstances or other people's decisions to hold me back.

I know I have things I need to work on, but I also recognize how far I have come.

## What I Really Want Out of Life

When I think about my future, I realize that what I want most is freedom of choice.

I want financial independence. I want to be able to pay my bills comfortably, own nice things, travel, enjoy experiences, and help the people I love without putting myself in a difficult position.

I want to wake up knowing that I am working toward something that belongs to me. I would love to own a successful business, invest, build multiple streams of income, or find a career that provides significant earning potential.

I don't necessarily want to spend my entire life working just to make someone else wealthy. I want to learn how money works, understand how to build wealth, and eventually create opportunities that allow my money and hard work to work for me.

I am not afraid of working hard. What I want is for that hard work to lead somewhere.
